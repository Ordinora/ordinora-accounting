# Client portal policy

The portal is a separate navigation and authorization surface. It is disabled for new tenants; when enabled, the default report mode is `PUBLISHED_ONLY` and payroll visibility is off.

Clients may view authorized dashboard cards and released reports, use authenticated downloads, upload source documents, answer visible questions, perform designated approvals, and inspect their own activity. They cannot create or modify journals, accounts, locks, statutory settings, tax workings, publications, internal notes, or another tenant's data.

`LIVE_POSTED` mode includes only posted entries and displays: “Figures reflect transactions posted as of [date and time] and may be subject to review, corrections and year-end adjustments.” Published versions show period, publication metadata, version, period state, note/disclaimer and last included posting. Superseded versions remain in audit history.

Payroll permissions are independent: aggregate, SPK summary, department, employee detail and payslip access are separate grants.
